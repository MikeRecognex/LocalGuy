Gemma 4's architectural improvements extend beyond English performance, with impressive results across European languages. [Benchmark data](https://www.reddit.com/gallery/1seo2rq) shows the 31B variant ranking in the top 3 across multiple languages: 1st place in Finnish, 2nd in Danish, French, and Italian, and 3rd in Dutch and English. This multilingual strength is a significant advantage for European users and organizations building locally-hosted applications for non-English markets.

For practitioners targeting international deployments, Gemma 4's balanced performance across language families makes it an excellent choice for self-hosted, privacy-conscious systems. The combination of reasonable parameter count (31B) and strong multilingual capabilities means organizations can deploy a single model globally without sacrificing performance for regional languages.
