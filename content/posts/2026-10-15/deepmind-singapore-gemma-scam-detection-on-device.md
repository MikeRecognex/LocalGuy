This collaboration showcases how fine-tuned open-source models can solve critical real-world problems while maintaining user privacy through on-device processing. The scam detection model runs entirely locally on smartphones, ensuring that sensitive communication data never leaves the user's device. DeepMind's approach to optimising Gemma for this task provides valuable insights into effective fine-tuning strategies for edge deployment, including model compression and latency optimisation without sacrificing detection accuracy.

The practical success of this deployment demonstrates that on-device AI isn't limited to generic tasks—highly specialised, domain-specific models can be efficiently run locally. This opens possibilities for similar applications in fraud detection, content moderation, and other safety-critical domains where privacy and latency are paramount considerations.
