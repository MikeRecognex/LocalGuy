Choosing appropriate hardware is the foundation of successful local LLM deployment, yet many practitioners struggle to match model sizes to available VRAM. This resource provides clear, practical guidance on GPU memory requirements for different Ollama model variants, helping teams make informed hardware purchasing decisions.

Understanding VRAM constraints—and knowing which GPUs offer the best value for inference workloads—is essential for cost-effective local deployment. The guide covers support for NVIDIA, AMD, and Apple Silicon hardware, enabling practitioners to optimize their infrastructure regardless of platform. This directly impacts deployment feasibility and operational costs for on-device inference projects.
