Ollama has announced a $65 million Series B funding round, marking a major milestone for the open-source local LLM movement. The platform, which simplifies running large language models on consumer hardware, has grown to nearly 9 million monthly active users—a testament to the growing demand for privacy-first, on-device AI inference.

This investment signals strong market validation for locally-deployed LLMs as an alternative to cloud-based AI APIs. With these resources, Ollama plans to expand its platform capabilities, improve performance across different hardware configurations, and build out ecosystem support. For practitioners running models on personal machines, this represents a critical validation that local inference is becoming mainstream infrastructure rather than a niche technical pursuit.

The funding also reflects broader industry momentum toward decentralized AI, where developers can maintain data privacy and reduce latency by keeping models on-device rather than relying on external API providers.
