Chat Privacy is a practical security tool that addresses a real-world deployment concern: how to safely use AI chat interfaces in shared-screen environments without exposing sensitive conversation context. The extension provides local filtering and masking of chat history, making it safe to present or record sessions containing proprietary information, credentials, or confidential discussions.

This is particularly relevant for organizations deploying local LLMs internally, where employees need collaborative tools but must maintain data governance standards. The local nature of the privacy controls (running entirely in the browser) means no additional servers or trust relationships are required—the protection happens at the endpoint.

[Install Chat Privacy](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/chat-privacy/piakgcdlbcadfnhgmpfpfejfmmpmnmkp) to add privacy controls to your AI conversations.
