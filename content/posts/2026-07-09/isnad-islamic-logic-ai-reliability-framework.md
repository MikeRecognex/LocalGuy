Isnad presents a novel approach to AI reliability by adapting historical verification methods—specifically the Islamic scholarly tradition of chain authentication (isnad)—into a Python framework for validating AI system behavior. The concept treats sources, transformations, and model outputs as a verifiable chain, where each step's provenance and reliability can be tracked and validated.

For teams deploying local LLMs in critical applications (healthcare, legal, finance), this framework offers a structured methodology for building confidence in model outputs. Rather than treating local LLM inference as a black box, Isnad enables practitioners to document and verify the chain of reasoning, model versions, input sources, and output decisions—creating auditable AI pipelines that meet regulatory and organizational trust requirements.
