vLLM has emerged as a critical framework for production inference serving, offering substantial performance improvements over traditional approaches through its innovative continuous batching architecture. This 2026 guide provides practitioners with the knowledge needed to deploy vLLM reliably in production settings, addressing scalability, monitoring, and operational concerns that arise beyond proof-of-concept implementations.

The guide covers essential production considerations including load balancing strategies, GPU memory management, request queuing optimization, and telemetry/monitoring setup. Readers will find practical guidance on troubleshooting common deployment challenges, configuring for different model sizes, and achieving high throughput while maintaining acceptable latency characteristics.

For organizations deploying local LLMs at scale, vLLM represents a mature choice that bridges the gap between research implementations and production requirements. Understanding how to properly configure and deploy vLLM enables teams to serve multiple models efficiently on limited hardware, reducing infrastructure costs while maintaining the latency and throughput characteristics required by production applications.
