Memory constraints remain one of the biggest barriers to local LLM deployment. This article highlights a practical approach to reducing VRAM consumption through targeted configuration adjustments, making it possible to run larger models on consumer hardware without expensive upgrades.

For practitioners running models on limited hardware—whether on laptops, edge devices, or older workstations—these optimization techniques could be the difference between viable and impossible deployments. The findings underscore that raw hardware specs aren't always the limiting factor; intelligent software tuning can unlock significant efficiency gains.

This type of practical optimization guidance is invaluable for the local LLM community, as it demonstrates concrete ways to maximize existing hardware capabilities without requiring quantisation or model distillation.
