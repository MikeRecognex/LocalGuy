MacBooks have emerged as one of the most accessible platforms for local LLM experimentation, combining powerful Apple Silicon with reasonable price points. However, choosing the right model for your RAM configuration requires understanding memory requirements, quantization trade-offs, and performance characteristics across different sizes.

This guide addresses a critical practical gap: most practitioners don't have clear guidance on whether their 16GB, 24GB, or larger MacBook configuration can run various model sizes effectively. By mapping model sizes (7B, 13B, 27B+) to RAM tiers and discussing quantization strategies (Q4, Q5, Q8), the guide helps users maximize their hardware without wasting time on incompatible configurations or leaving performance on the table.

With MLX bringing native Apple Silicon optimizations and models being released at increasingly diverse parameter counts, this type of pragmatic hardware-to-model matching becomes essential reference material for the growing community of developers deploying locally on Macs.
