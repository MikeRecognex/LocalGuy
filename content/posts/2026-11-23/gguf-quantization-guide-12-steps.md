GGUF (GGLM Unified Format) has become the standard format for distributing quantized LLMs optimized for local inference. This guide breaks down the quantization process into 12 actionable steps, making it accessible for practitioners looking to convert and optimize their own models for edge deployment.

Quantization is critical for local LLM deployment as it reduces model size and memory requirements while maintaining reasonable inference quality. By understanding the GGUF format and mastering the quantization workflow, developers can prepare models for deployment on resource-constrained devices like laptops, embedded systems, and mobile hardware.

The systematic approach outlined here bridges the gap between raw model weights and production-ready quantized artifacts that work with popular inference engines like llama.cpp and Ollama.
