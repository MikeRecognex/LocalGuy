Qualcomm's Snapdragon Reality Elite chipset represents hardware increasingly optimized specifically for local AI workloads. Rather than relying on general-purpose processors, specialized AI accelerators and NPUs (Neural Processing Units) enable efficient inference without cloud dependencies, crucial for privacy-sensitive and latency-critical applications.

This hardware evolution matters significantly for local LLM practitioners because it expands the viable deployment surface. As mobile and edge chipsets gain dedicated AI capabilities, running models locally becomes more feasible on consumer devices, not just high-end workstations or servers.

The combination of mixed reality and on-device AI suggests an emerging class of applications where local inference is essential—applications that can't tolerate cloud latency or network dependencies. This drives optimization work in quantisation, efficient architectures, and frameworks that can target Snapdragon's specific accelerators.
