As more organizations deploy LLMs locally without direct vendor oversight, [topological control approaches](https://cacm.acm.org/blogcacm/topological-control-of-llms-a-route-to-trustworthy-ai/) become increasingly important for ensuring model safety and reliability. This research provides technical foundations for building trustworthy systems with locally-hosted models.

For practitioners deploying language models on-device or self-hosted, understanding how to control and verify model behavior is critical. Topological methods offer a principled way to ensure that models behave predictably and safely without requiring external API validation or cloud-based safety checks.

This research bridges the gap between model freedom and responsible deployment, enabling organizations to confidently run powerful models locally while maintaining the safety guarantees they need for production environments.
