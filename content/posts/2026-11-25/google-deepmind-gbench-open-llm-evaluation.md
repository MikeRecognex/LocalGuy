Practical local LLM deployment requires principled decision-making about which models to run. gBench, now freely available from Google DeepMind, provides a standardized evaluation framework that moves beyond cherry-picked metrics and anecdotal impressions. The tool enables practitioners to systematically compare model capabilities across realistic use cases, essential for understanding tradeoffs between model size, latency, and quality.

For teams self-hosting models, this removes much of the guesswork from model selection. Rather than manually testing each candidate model on your specific workloads, gBench provides reproducible benchmarks that highlight genuine performance differences. This is particularly valuable as the ecosystem of edge-optimized models continues expanding—having neutral, open evaluation infrastructure helps the community collectively understand which models actually deliver on their efficiency promises.
