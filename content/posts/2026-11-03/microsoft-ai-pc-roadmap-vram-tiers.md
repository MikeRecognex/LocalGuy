Microsoft's published AI PC roadmap provides crucial clarity on hardware requirements for different local inference scenarios. The tiered approach—8GB for basic capabilities, 16GB to qualify for Copilot+, and up to 128GB for local supercomputing—establishes realistic expectations for practitioners deploying LLMs across consumer hardware.

This roadmap validates the importance of quantization, model distillation, and efficient inference frameworks for making AI practical on constrained devices. With 16GB becoming the "sufficient" tier for modern Copilot+ features, it reinforces the value of techniques like 4-bit quantization and selective layer offloading that enable 7-13B models to run smoothly on mainstream laptops. The 128GB tier recognition signals enterprise interest in local inference for sensitive workloads, justifying continued optimization work in frameworks like vLLM and llama.cpp for high-memory deployment scenarios.
