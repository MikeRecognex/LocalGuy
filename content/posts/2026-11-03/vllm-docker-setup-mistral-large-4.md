Deploying large language models locally can be complex, but containerization with Docker significantly simplifies the process. This guide breaks down the vLLM setup for Mistral Large 4 into 13 manageable steps, making it accessible for developers who want to self-host this capable model without wrestling with dependency management or system configuration issues.

VLLM's optimized inference engine combined with Docker's reproducibility makes this an ideal approach for production-grade local deployments. The containerized approach ensures consistency across different development and deployment environments, while vLLM provides performance optimizations like continuous batching and KV-cache management that are essential for efficient inference at scale.
