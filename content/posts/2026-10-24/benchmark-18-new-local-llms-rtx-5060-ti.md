A detailed benchmark comparing 18 recent local LLM releases on dual RTX 5060 Ti GPUs reveals important performance baselines for practitioners working with affordable consumer hardware. The test methodology focuses on real-world constraints—modest VRAM budgets typical of gaming-class GPUs—and provides data on inference speed, quality, and practical usability across the latest model releases.

The finding that none of the newer models displaced Qwen 27B as the performance leader is particularly valuable information for practitioners making hardware investment decisions. This suggests that rapid model proliferation in the open-source space hasn't yet delivered across-the-board improvements on the specific constraints of local deployment. The benchmark serves as a reality check against marketing claims and provides concrete data for practitioners choosing between competing models and quantization strategies.

For anyone considering RTX 5060 Ti as a value-oriented platform for local inference, this benchmark is essential reference material. It documents actual achievable performance with current models and provides a baseline against which to measure future improvements or new model releases in this VRAM-constrained category.
