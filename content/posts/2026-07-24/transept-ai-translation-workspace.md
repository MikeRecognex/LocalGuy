Transept introduces a human-first approach to AI-powered translation, offering a workspace designed to keep professionals in control of the translation process. The platform exemplifies how thoughtfully designed interfaces can maximize the value of local or hybrid LLM deployments in professional contexts.

[Transept's approach](https://transept.ai) prioritizes human judgment while leveraging AI capabilities for speed and consistency. This model is particularly valuable for organizations wanting to deploy language models locally while maintaining quality control and professional oversight.

For practitioners interested in building application layers around local LLMs, Transept demonstrates best practices in human-AI collaboration, showing how on-device models can enhance rather than replace human expertise in specialized domains.
