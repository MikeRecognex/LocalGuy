SAP has introduced round-trip correctness, a new evaluation metric specifically designed for generative AI-based process modeling. This metric addresses a critical gap in how we measure the reliability and correctness of AI outputs, particularly important for practitioners deploying models in production environments.

[Round-trip correctness](https://community.sap.com/t5/technology-blog-posts-by-sap/round-trip-correctness-a-new-metric-for-generative-ai-based-process/ba-p/14091979) evaluates whether AI-generated outputs can be correctly interpreted and processed back through the system without degradation. This is especially relevant for local LLM deployments where model behavior directly impacts downstream applications.

For teams running models on-device, understanding and implementing proper evaluation metrics helps ensure model quality before deployment and provides confidence in production systems. This metric contributes to the growing ecosystem of tools for benchmarking and validating local inference quality.
