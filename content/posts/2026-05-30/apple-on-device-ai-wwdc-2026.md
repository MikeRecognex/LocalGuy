Apple is placing on-device AI at the center of its WWDC 2026 strategy, reinforcing its commitment to running inference locally on iPhones, iPads, and Macs rather than relying on cloud APIs. This represents a fundamental architectural choice that could influence how developers approach LLM deployment across Apple's ecosystem, with potential implications for privacy-conscious applications and performance optimisation on Apple Silicon.

The emphasis on on-device intelligence addresses growing concerns about data privacy and latency in AI applications. By processing language models locally—likely leveraging Apple Neural Engine and the company's optimised frameworks—users retain full control over sensitive information. This approach aligns with emerging regulations like the EU AI Act and GDPR, making it an attractive model for enterprise deployments.

For developers targeting Apple platforms, this strategic focus means investing in knowledge of Core ML, Metal Performance Shaders, and quantisation techniques specifically tuned for Apple Silicon.
