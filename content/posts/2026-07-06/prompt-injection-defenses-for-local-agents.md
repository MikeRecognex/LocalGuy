As LLM agents become more prevalent in local deployments, prompt-injection attacks represent a critical security consideration that practitioners cannot ignore. This survey provides a thorough examination of existing defense mechanisms and clarifies that while various approaches exist, no single solution has definitively solved the problem.

For teams building local agentic systems—particularly those that interact with user input, web content, or untrusted data sources—understanding the threat model and available defenses is fundamental. The survey's analysis of different defense strategies helps practitioners make informed decisions about which approach best fits their deployment context and risk tolerance.
