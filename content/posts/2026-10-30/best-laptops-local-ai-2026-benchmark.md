Hardware selection fundamentally determines the feasibility and performance characteristics of local LLM deployments. StorageReview's lab-tested leaderboard moves beyond theoretical specifications to provide real-world performance data across different laptop configurations, eliminating guesswork from hardware purchasing decisions.

The 2026 benchmark likely reflects the increasing maturity of Apple Silicon, AMD's RDNA improvements, and Intel's latest architectures for local inference. Understanding which specific hardware configurations achieve acceptable latency and throughput for different model sizes and quantization levels is essential for practitioners planning deployments—whether for development, edge deployment, or production use.
