Ollama's latest update adds native text-to-speech functionality directly to the platform, enabling users to generate natural-sounding speech without relying on cloud APIs. This represents a significant expansion of what local LLM deployments can accomplish, moving beyond text generation into multimodal inference.

The integration of TTS with Ollama on macOS makes it practical for developers building end-to-end local AI applications—from chatbots to accessibility tools—all running on consumer hardware. This addition addresses a critical gap in the open-source local inference ecosystem where TTS traditionally required external services or complex workarounds.

For practitioners running private AI systems, this is particularly valuable as it removes another dependency on external services and keeps all data processing on-device. Combined with recent improvements to model compatibility across platforms, Ollama continues to solidify its position as the most accessible local LLM runtime.
