Unsloth has announced Unsloth Studio, a new open-source web UI designed to streamline both training and inference of LLMs on local hardware. Available under the Apache license, the tool integrates seamlessly with the llama.cpp ecosystem and offers a unified interface for managing models—a significant development for practitioners who've relied on LMStudio as their primary GGUF runner.

This release is particularly notable because it lowers the barrier to entry for users wanting to both fine-tune and deploy models locally. The project is [available on GitHub](https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth), and early community reception has been exceptionally positive (780+ upvotes), suggesting strong demand for alternatives to proprietary solutions. For teams with local inference infrastructure, Unsloth Studio could reduce operational complexity by consolidating training and serving workflows.

The open-source nature and llama.cpp compatibility make this especially relevant for edge deployments and organizations seeking to avoid cloud vendor lock-in while maintaining full control over their model lifecycle.
