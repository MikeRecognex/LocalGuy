The convergence of wireless connectivity and local AI capabilities on a single low-power chip marks a significant inflection point for edge deployment. Nordic's nRF71 Series brings on-device AI inference to battery-powered IoT devices while maintaining 20x lower standby power than previous generations—a critical metric for always-on applications.

This category of hardware represents the frontier of local LLM deployment, extending inference beyond traditional compute-rich platforms to embedded systems. For applications requiring immediate, continuous AI inference—security monitoring, real-time analytics, autonomous sensors—these integrated solutions eliminate cloud dependency entirely. The combination of Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Thread connectivity with local processing capability opens new classes of always-connected yet privacy-preserving applications.
