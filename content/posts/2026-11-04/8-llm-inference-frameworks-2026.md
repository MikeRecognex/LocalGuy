As local LLM deployment matures, the ecosystem of inference frameworks has expanded significantly. This roundup evaluates leading options that enable efficient on-device model execution, helping practitioners navigate choices between established projects like llama.cpp and Ollama versus emerging alternatives.

Whether targeting consumer hardware, edge devices, or server-class systems, selecting the right inference engine is critical for achieving production-grade performance.
