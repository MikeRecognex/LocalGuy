Real-world performance data comparing local inference against cloud services is essential for practitioners evaluating the viability of on-device deployment. This benchmark tests actual workflows across 20 tasks on consumer-grade Apple Silicon, providing concrete metrics on latency, throughput, and capability gaps versus cloud alternatives.

Such benchmarks matter because they move beyond synthetic metrics to show whether local solutions genuinely meet production requirements. For many applications—particularly those with privacy concerns, latency sensitivity, or cost constraints—local inference on well-equipped consumer hardware proves competitive, though trade-offs remain in model capability and specialized tasks.
