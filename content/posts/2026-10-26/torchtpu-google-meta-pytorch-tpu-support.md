At PyTorch Conference 2026, Google and Meta announced TorchTPU, an open-source framework enabling native PyTorch code execution on TPU hardware. This collaboration marks a significant expansion of the local inference ecosystem by providing first-class TPU support, which previously required custom integrations or workarounds.

TPUs have historically been accessible primarily through cloud providers, but TorchTPU's release enables on-premise TPU deployments and edge scenarios where organizations have TPU hardware available. For practitioners with access to TPU infrastructure, this means running PyTorch models directly on TPUs without performance penalties or architecture-specific optimizations.

The open-source nature of TorchTPU democratizes TPU access for the local inference community. Combined with existing NVIDIA GPU and Apple Silicon support in the PyTorch ecosystem, this creates a more diverse hardware landscape for on-device deployment. Organizations can now choose between CPU, GPU, and TPU backends with consistent PyTorch APIs.
