Asus's ExpertBook B3 G2, equipped with 50 TOPS of dedicated AI compute capability, represents a significant milestone in enterprise hardware for local model deployment. This class of business laptop brings meaningful acceleration for inference workloads, making on-device model serving viable for professional applications without requiring external GPU clusters.

With 50 TOPS available from integrated NPUs, practitioners can now run moderately-sized models with acceptable latency directly on standard enterprise hardware. This development is particularly significant for regulated industries where data residency and privacy requirements mandate local processing. The integration of powerful AI compute into commodity business laptops removes a major barrier to enterprise adoption of local LLM applications.

For organizations evaluating hardware refresh cycles, devices like the ExpertBook B3 G2 now offer compelling economics for local inference, reducing reliance on cloud API costs while improving data privacy and latency characteristics.
