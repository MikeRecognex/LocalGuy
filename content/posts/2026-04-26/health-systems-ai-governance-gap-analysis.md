Governance and compliance are increasingly critical concerns for local LLM deployments in regulated sectors. [The Enterprise Mobile AI Report for 2026](https://mobile.wednesday.is/the-enterprise-mobile-ai-report-2026) reveals a significant gap: while 75% of US health systems have deployed AI systems, only 18% maintain proper governance frameworks for those deployments.

This finding carries profound implications for practitioners building local LLM solutions in healthcare and other regulated industries. Self-hosted and edge-based inference systems can address many compliance requirements—data residency, audit trails, model reproducibility—but only when paired with appropriate governance architecture. The missing piece isn't technical capability; it's organizational structure and process maturity.

For teams deploying local LLMs in healthcare, finance, or other regulated environments, this research underscores the importance of establishing governance frameworks alongside technical infrastructure. Local deployment provides the foundation for compliance, but practitioners must explicitly implement monitoring, documentation, and oversight mechanisms that validate model behavior and decision-making.
