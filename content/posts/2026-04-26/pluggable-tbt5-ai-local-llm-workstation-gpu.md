Pluggable has launched the TBT5-AI, the first Thunderbolt 5 docking station explicitly designed for local LLM workstations. This represents hardware-level recognition of the local LLM market, enabling users to add high-speed GPU connectivity to laptops and compact workstations without major infrastructure changes.

Thunderbolt 5's 240 Gbps bandwidth provides sufficient throughput for modern GPUs, making it practical to attach discrete graphics for local inference tasks. The TBT5-AI's explicit focus on LLM workloads—rather than being a general-purpose dock—suggests manufacturers are recognizing meaningful demand from practitioners who need flexible, portable inference hardware.

For local LLM enthusiasts, the [Pluggable TBT5-AI](https://www.msn.com/en-us/tech/pluggable-tbt5-ai) represents a turning point: moving from makeshift solutions to purpose-built hardware. This accessibility matters because it lowers barriers to entry for running capable models locally, especially for those who want flexibility without committing to a fixed workstation setup.
