This analysis provides crucial economic grounding for the local vs. cloud LLM deployment decision. By breaking down the actual costs of running vLLM on owned hardware—including often-overlooked factors like electricity consumption and VRAM requirements—it gives practitioners concrete data to make TCO decisions. The comparison against Ollama's $20 monthly cloud tier is particularly timely as the ecosystem increasingly offers both managed and self-hosted options.

For organizations evaluating whether to invest in local inference infrastructure, this kind of granular cost analysis is essential. The conclusion often depends on usage patterns, available hardware, and organizational constraints around data residency. By examining power costs alongside subscription pricing, the analysis acknowledges that the true cost of local inference extends beyond model serving to infrastructure operations—a reality many practitioners underestimate when planning their deployments.
