[SourceHut reported serious infrastructure disruptions caused by LLM crawlers](https://status.sr.ht/issues/2026-06-06-llms-again/), highlighting the growing tension between AI model training and open-source communities. This incident demonstrates the material impact that large-scale data collection for LLM training has on public infrastructure and services.

For local LLM practitioners, this serves as an important reminder about data sourcing and ethical considerations when fine-tuning or pre-training models on collected data. The disruption underscores how uncontrolled scraping and data collection create real costs for infrastructure providers and open-source maintainers who support the ecosystem.

This trend may accelerate interest in locally-curated, ethically-sourced training data and fine-tuning approaches that work with specific domain datasets rather than broad web scraping, making it increasingly important for practitioners to be thoughtful about their data collection and usage practices.
