The llama.cpp community has released a dedicated benchmark dashboard tracking inference performance across AMD Ryzen processors and Radeon GPUs. This resource addresses a long-standing gap in publicly available performance data for AMD-based local deployments, complementing existing benchmarks that historically favoured NVIDIA and Apple Silicon hardware.

The dashboard enables practitioners to make informed hardware purchasing decisions for on-device inference by comparing throughput, latency, and power efficiency metrics across different model sizes and quantisation formats. With AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ and upcoming RDNA 4 architecture gaining traction for local AI workloads, transparent benchmarking is essential for the ecosystem. The dashboard covers both CPU-only inference paths and GPU-accelerated execution via Radeon RDNA architecture, filling a critical gap for developers targeting cost-effective AMD deployment scenarios.

This initiative reflects llama.cpp's maturation as a cross-platform inference engine and AMD's commitment to competitive local LLM performance. Regular benchmark updates will help the community track optimisation improvements and guide architecture-specific tuning decisions.
