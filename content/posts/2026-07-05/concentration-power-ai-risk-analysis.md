The article "[Concentration of Power in AI Is a Risk](https://xcancel.com/i/article/2072830533739192560)" by Andy Konwinski articulates the systemic risks created when AI capabilities concentrate in a small number of proprietary systems and cloud providers. This perspective provides important validation for the local LLM community's mission to democratize access to capable AI models.

For practitioners and organizations, local LLM deployment represents both a technical strategy and a risk mitigation approach. By maintaining capability to run models on-device and on-premise, teams reduce dependency on external services, improve privacy, ensure availability, and gain control over their AI infrastructure. These benefits extend beyond convenience to touching on organizational resilience and independence.

As regulatory pressure and geopolitical considerations around AI increase, the ability to deploy and operate models locally becomes increasingly valuable. Local deployment strategies provide a hedge against service disruptions, policy changes, and vendor lock-in scenarios that could impact AI-dependent workflows.
