This comprehensive guide addresses one of the most frequently asked questions in local LLM deployment: what hardware do I actually need? The Hostinger resource breaks down GPU VRAM requirements, system RAM needs, and which GPUs are officially supported by Ollama, providing essential infrastructure planning information for anyone looking to deploy models locally.

Understanding these specifications is critical for cost-effective deployment. The guide helps practitioners avoid over-provisioning hardware while ensuring they have sufficient resources to run their target models smoothly. With Ollama's growing adoption as the de facto standard for easy local inference, having authoritative documentation on hardware requirements reduces barriers to entry and helps community members make data-driven decisions about their hardware investments.

For teams evaluating on-device inference, this resource serves as a practical starting point for capacity planning, supporting decision-making around whether to use consumer GPUs, server-grade accelerators, or CPU-only inference, depending on throughput and latency requirements.
