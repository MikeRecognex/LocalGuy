The MLX ecosystem continues to mature with tools that make it easier for macOS users to deploy and manage local LLMs on Apple Silicon. The Control Center provides a user-friendly interface for what has traditionally required command-line expertise, lowering the barrier to entry for running models locally on Apple devices.

For the growing segment of developers using Apple Silicon Macs, MLX remains the most natural choice for local inference, offering native performance advantages without cross-platform compatibility concerns. Regular updates like v0.4 demonstrate active development and community investment in making local LLM deployment more accessible and user-friendly on the macOS platform.
