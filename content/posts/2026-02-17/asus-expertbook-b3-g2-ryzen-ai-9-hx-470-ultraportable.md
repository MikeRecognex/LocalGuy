ASUS has unveiled the ExpertBook B3 G2, an ultraportable laptop that combines the powerful Ryzen AI 9 HX 470 processor with an impressively light 1.41kg chassis. The Ryzen AI 9 HX 470 includes specialized NPU (Neural Processing Unit) capabilities that dramatically accelerate local AI inference, making this device particularly attractive for developers and professionals requiring on-device LLM execution without cloud dependency.

The integration of AMD's latest AI-focused silicon into an ultraportable form factor represents a meaningful advancement for local inference practitioners. The NPU acceleration can significantly improve inference speed and reduce latency for compatible models, while the lightweight design makes it practical for deployment in mobile and field scenarios. This hardware trend aligns with the broader industry shift toward edge AI, where inference capabilities are increasingly embedded into consumer-grade devices.

For local LLM practitioners, ultraportable laptops with specialized AI accelerators offer a compelling platform for running production models while maintaining mobility. The ExpertBook B3 G2 demonstrates that [high-performance local inference is increasingly accessible](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/technology/asus-expertbook-b3-g2-laptop-has-a-ryzen-ai-9-hx-470-cpu-in-a-1-41kg-body/ar-AA1svmN0) in practical, deployable form factors.
