One of the primary barriers to local LLM adoption is uncertainty about hardware adequacy. Many developers don't know whether their existing PC can handle local inference efficiently, leading them to default to cloud solutions. This guide addresses exactly that pain point by cataloging free diagnostic tools that measure AI inference capability against consumer hardware—enabling informed decisions before model deployment.

Capability assessment is essential infrastructure for the local LLM ecosystem. By providing practitioners with transparent, easy-to-use measurement tools, more developers can make data-driven choices about local vs. cloud deployment rather than making assumptions about their hardware limitations. This democratizes access to local inference and helps identify which hardware configurations are genuinely viable for different use cases.
