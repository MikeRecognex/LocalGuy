This development underscores a critical advantage of truly local, self-hosted LLM deployments: complete transparency and control over data handling. When vendors remove privacy assurances from their "on-device" offerings, it demonstrates why many practitioners prefer deploying open-source models themselves rather than relying on proprietary vendors' claims about data protection.

For organizations and individuals committed to privacy, this serves as a timely reminder that local LLM deployment—whether via Ollama, llama.cpp, or other frameworks—provides the only auditable, fully controllable approach to AI inference. Self-hosted solutions eliminate ambiguity about where data flows and who can access it, making local deployment not just a technical choice but a governance requirement for privacy-conscious workloads.
