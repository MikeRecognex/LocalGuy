AIWatermarkDetector is an open-source tool that identifies watermarks embedded in AI-generated text and code—important for understanding the provenance and authenticity of content in local development workflows. As local LLM deployments become more common, the ability to detect and analyze AI-generated artifacts becomes increasingly relevant for compliance, attribution, and quality assurance purposes.

For teams running local coding assistants or content generation systems, this tool provides visibility into whether outputs contain detectable watermarks from upstream model providers or training processes. This can inform decisions about model selection, fine-tuning, and integration with downstream tools. While watermarking itself is still an evolving area, having detection capabilities available helps practitioners understand the technical landscape and make informed choices about their deployment architectures.

[The detector is available on GitHub](https://github.com/ulrischa/AIWatermarkDetector) and can be integrated into local workflows, CI/CD pipelines, or content validation systems to maintain transparency about AI-generated content sources.
