The arrival of Apple's M5 Max with 128GB of unified memory represents a significant milestone for local LLM deployment on consumer hardware. With substantially increased memory capacity compared to previous generations, practitioners can now run larger models with better performance characteristics directly on MacBook Pro systems.

The community is actively benchmarking the M5 Max to establish performance baselines across different quantization levels and model sizes. This data will be crucial for developers deciding whether to target Apple Silicon as a platform for local inference, particularly for knowledge workers and professionals who need privacy-preserving AI capabilities on portable hardware.

Early indications suggest the M5 Max could become a go-to device for running models in the 30-70B parameter range locally, filling an important gap between smaller edge devices and full datacenter deployments.
