Understanding the practical strengths and limitations of locally-deployed models is crucial for making informed architecture decisions. Not every use case is equally suited to local deployment, and this honest assessment helps practitioners avoid costly mistakes and misaligned expectations.

Local LLMs excel at focused, well-defined tasks with controlled inputs: code completion, summarization, classification, structured data extraction, and domain-specific question-answering where you have fine-tuning data. They struggle with open-ended reasoning, up-to-date information retrieval, and complex multi-step planning that requires broad world knowledge. The key is matching model capabilities to your specific use case rather than trying to use local models as drop-in replacements for larger, cloud-hosted alternatives.

Read the full analysis at [How-To Geek](https://www.howtogeek.com) to understand which workloads are ideal candidates for local deployment and which might benefit from a hybrid approach combining local and cloud-based models.
