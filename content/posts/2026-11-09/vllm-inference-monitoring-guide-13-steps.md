Monitoring is often the overlooked component in local LLM deployments, yet it's critical for production readiness. This structured guide provides a 13-step framework for implementing comprehensive monitoring of vLLM-based inference systems within 60 minutes. Coverage includes performance metrics, resource utilisation tracking, and observability infrastructure essential for maintaining stable local deployment environments.

The guide addresses the practical gap between proof-of-concept and production deployment. Many organisations successfully run LLMs locally but lack visibility into token throughput, latency patterns, GPU utilisation efficiency, and error rates. These metrics are essential for optimisation decisions, capacity planning, and identifying bottlenecks before they impact production workloads.

For practitioners moving LLM inference in-house, monitoring infrastructure determines whether deployments scale reliably or degrade unpredictably. This guide accelerates the time-to-production by condensing operational best practices into actionable steps, enabling teams to instrument their vLLM deployments with minimal overhead and start gathering meaningful performance data immediately.
