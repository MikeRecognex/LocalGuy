Choosing the right GPU is one of the most critical decisions when building a local LLM deployment, yet it remains confusing due to the wide array of options, driver support variations, and poorly documented real-world performance characteristics. This guide addresses a genuine pain point in the community by providing practical selection criteria rather than abstract specifications.

The framing of 'fast, free, and unlimited tokens' directly speaks to the core advantages of local inference over cloud APIs—no per-token costs, predictable latency, and complete control over resource allocation. For practitioners evaluating hardware investments, understanding the performance-per-dollar ratio across different GPU tiers is essential to avoid over-provisioning or selecting hardware that bottlenecks model inference.

This kind of practical guidance is especially valuable as newer GPUs continue entering the market and different architectures (NVIDIA, AMD) offer increasingly competitive options for local deployment.
