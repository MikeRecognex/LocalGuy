Spiceworks has published a detailed technical analysis of Ollama, examining the platform's hardware requirements, supported model formats, and security implications for self-hosted LLM deployment. The analysis clarifies which hardware (GPUs, Apple Silicon, etc.) provides optimal performance for different Ollama use cases, which models are most compatible and efficient, and what security considerations teams should evaluate before deploying Ollama in production environments.

For IT teams and developers evaluating Ollama as a local inference platform, this comprehensive guide addresses the practical questions: What GPU should we buy? How much VRAM do we need? Are there supply chain or data privacy concerns? Ollama has become the most accessible entry point for local LLM deployment thanks to its simple installation and model management, but understanding hardware requirements and security posture is critical for enterprise adoption. The guide helps teams make informed decisions about whether Ollama fits their infrastructure, security, and compliance requirements.
