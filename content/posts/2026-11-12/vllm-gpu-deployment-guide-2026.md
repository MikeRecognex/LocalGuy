Hostinger has published a comprehensive deployment guide for running LLMs with vLLM on GPU servers in 2026. The guide addresses practical questions around infrastructure setup, model selection, performance tuning, and production considerations for self-hosted inference. vLLM's PagedAttention mechanism and continuous batching capabilities have made it the go-to inference engine for production deployments requiring high throughput.

This guide is essential for teams graduating from local development to production inference. While local laptop development is valuable for experimentation, deployment on GPU servers requires understanding memory management, batching strategies, and cost optimization. The guide likely covers vLLM's latest features and best practices for common scenarios: running open-weight models at scale, handling variable sequence lengths efficiently, and managing GPU utilization. For organizations building AI products with local/self-hosted infrastructure, proper vLLM deployment is often the bridge between prototype and production.
