One of the strongest arguments for local LLM deployment just got quantified: running your own models on consumer hardware costs less per month than a single subscription to closed-source APIs. This analysis measured real-world power consumption and calculated actual electricity costs, providing concrete ROI metrics for on-premise inference.

For organizations and power users evaluating deployment strategies, this data strengthens the economic case for self-hosting. When combined with privacy benefits and inference latency improvements, the total cost of ownership for local deployment becomes increasingly attractive, especially for consistent, high-volume usage patterns where API per-token costs would accumulate rapidly.
