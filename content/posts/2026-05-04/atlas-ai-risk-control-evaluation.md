Running LLMs locally means taking full responsibility for safety, security, and reliability—and [Atlas addresses this with ready-to-deploy evaluation frameworks](https://atlas.latticeflow.ai/). The tool provides pre-built assessments that help local operators understand and mitigate risks in their models before they reach users.

For self-hosted deployments, Atlas reduces the barrier to implementing production-grade quality assurance. Instead of building evaluation pipelines from scratch, developers can leverage existing frameworks designed by the community, accelerating time-to-deployment and ensuring consistent safety standards.

This is especially important for local LLM practitioners operating in regulated industries or handling sensitive applications, where evaluation rigor directly impacts compliance and user trust.
